AI Study Assistant

Paste your notes — get a quiz, flashcards, a study plan and a focus timer. Study smarter, not longer.

by Sufyan

Quiz Maker

Write or paste your notes below, one fact per line like Term: definition sentence. Then press Generate Quiz — the assistant picks the key word out of every sentence and turns it into a question.

Flashcards

Flashcards are made from the same notes (the part before the colon becomes the front of the card). Click the card to flip it.

Press the button to make your cards

Study Plan

Tell the assistant your subject, your topics (one per line) and how many days you have. It will spread the topics over your days and keep the last day for revision and a self test.

Focus Timer

Study in focused rounds: 25 minutes of study, then a 5 minute break. Press start and put the phone away.
